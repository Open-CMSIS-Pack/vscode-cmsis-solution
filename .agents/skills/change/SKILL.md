# Change

1. Read and follow the [local overlay instructions](../../instructions/local-overlays.md).
2. Read and follow the [development principles](../../instructions/principles.md).
3. Read and follow the [file-formatting instructions](../../instructions/formatting.md).
4. For TypeScript changes, follow the [TypeScript instructions](../../instructions/typescript.md).
5. When adding or changing tests, follow the [unit-test instructions](../../instructions/unit-tests.md).
6. Consult the [architecture](../../references/architecture.md), [structure](../../references/structure.md), and [module map](../../references/modules.md) only as needed.
7. Follow the approved plan when one exists.
8. Make the smallest complete change that satisfies the request.
9. Run focused validation after editing.
