# Change

1. Read and follow the [local overlay instructions](../../instructions/local-overlays.md).
2. Read and follow the [development principles](../../instructions/principles.md).
3. Read and follow the [file-formatting instructions](../../instructions/formatting.md).
4. For TypeScript changes, follow the [TypeScript instructions](../../instructions/typescript.md).
5. Assess coverage for testable changes; follow the [test-coverage skill](../test-coverage/SKILL.md) when writing or substantially changing unit tests.
6. Follow the [structured-files skill](../structured-files/SKILL.md) when implementing JSON, YAML, or XML parsing, creation, or updates.
7. Consult the [architecture](../../references/architecture.md), [structure](../../references/structure.md), and [module map](../../references/modules.md) only as needed.
8. Follow the approved plan when one exists.
9. Make the smallest complete change that satisfies the request.
10. Run focused validation after editing.
